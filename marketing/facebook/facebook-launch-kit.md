## 2. First posts

Post these over two weeks, two a week. The first one matters most.

### Post 1 — the story (day one)
**Image:** a real photo — the paperback Kookbook, or Poppie at the stove. *(Not in this folder: only you have it.)*

> My dad cooked for our family my whole life. When we moved to Jersey and he stayed in South Africa, that was the part I missed most.
>
> My daughter Bella asked Poppie to write his recipes down. He made us a paperback — Poppie's Kookbook — and now the kids cook his food too.
>
> This app started from that book. It's a place for your family's recipes: the ones everyone asks for, the ones only one person knows how to make, the ones worth passing down.

> Pick three recipes for the week and the shopping list writes itself — and as of this week, the same food from different dishes lands on one line. Eggs for the tart and eggs for the stir-fry just say "Egg · 6".

### Post 6 — bring the family in
**Image:** `cover-photo.png`

>
> Whoever starts the cookbook can invite the people they cook for — grandparents, kids, the cousin in another country — and they join free. Everyone adds their own recipes, notes and tweaks, and it all ends up in one place.

## 3. What makes it work

- **Share post 1 from your own profile** and into groups you're already in (school parents, Jersey and South African community groups) — that's where a new page's first few hundred people come from. Ask your friend what did it for Jersey Beach; do that again.
- **Real photos beat app screenshots.** Poppie, the paperback, the kids cooking, food on the actual table.
- **Answer every comment on post 2.** Questions only grow a page if someone answers.
- **Two posts a week is plenty.** Consistency matters more than volume.
